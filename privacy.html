Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who we are

Inside Human is an educational channel run by an individual creator, reachable through the YouTube channel. This policy covers this website and the private tool Inside Human Uploader.

This website

This site is static. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms, and it does not collect personal information.

Inside Human Uploader (YouTube API)

Inside Human Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Inside Human channel to upload the channel's own videos. It uses the YouTube API scope youtube.upload to upload videos, titles, descriptions and thumbnails to the owner's own channel.

  • Data accessed: only the authorisation granted by the channel owner's own Google account.
  • Storage: the authorisation token is stored locally on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any third party and not shared.
  • Other users: the tool does not collect, store or process data from any other user or viewer.
  • Revocation: access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account permissions page: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Inside Human Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Third-party services

Videos are published on YouTube and TikTok. Their own privacy policies apply to anything you do on those platforms.
