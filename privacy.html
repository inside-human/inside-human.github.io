Privacy Policy

LoPeor Publisher · Last updated: November 15, 2026

1. Overview

LoPeor Publisher is a personal tool used by one individual (the "Operator") to publish the Operator's own videos to the Operator's own TikTok account. It does not collect data from the public, has no user registration, and does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. Data we access

When the Operator authorizes the app with TikTok Login Kit, the app receives:

  • basic profile information of the authorized account (open ID, display name), through the user.info.basic scope;
  • an access token and a refresh token, used only to post videos to that account, through the video.publish and video.upload scopes.

3. How data is used

Data is used only to upload and publish the Operator's videos and to check the status of those uploads. It is not used for profiling, advertising or any other purpose.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored in a protected file on the Operator's own computer, with restricted file permissions. They are never published, committed to a code repository or shared. Videos are stored on the Operator's own devices before upload.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok, as needed to perform the uploads.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the authorization is active. The Operator can revoke access at any time from TikTok's account settings (Security and permissions → Apps and services), and deletes the stored tokens when access is revoked.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not used by anyone other than the Operator.
